## 1. Host Infrastructure & Cluster Deployment

1. **Host Environment:**
   - The primary execution cluster is running under **WSL2 Ubuntu 24.04 LTS** on the developer host, utilizing native Big Data daemons:
     - **Apache Hadoop 3.3.6:** Pseudo-distributed single-node HDFS (RPC on port 9000, WebUI on port 9870) and YARN NodeManager/ResourceManager (port 8088).
     - **Apache Hive 4.0.1:** Hive Metastore and HiveServer2 (listening on port 10000 via PyHive and Beeline).
     - **Apache Pig 0.17.0:** Execution engine on MapReduce (`pig -x mapreduce`).
     - **MongoDB 8.0.32:** Document database for unstructured intake (port 27017).
     - **Python 3.12:** Ingestion workers, PyHive/PyMongo connectors, and Flask web framework.
2. **Containerization Equivalence:**
   - A fully functional `docker-compose.yml` is provided at the repository root defining Hadoop NameNode, DataNode, HiveServer2, Hive Metastore, and MongoDB for zero-configuration containerized deployments on Docker-enabled workstations.

## 2. Real vs. Synthetic Data Allocation

1. **Real Data (Kaggle Dataset):**
   - The real dataset is exclusively sourced from [Rohan Rao's Air Quality Data in India (2015–2020)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rohanrao/air-quality-data-in-india).
   - Extracted directly into `data/raw/`:
     - `stations.csv`: 230 CPCB station records.
     - `station_hour.csv`: Hourly station-level observations for real-time replay.
     - `station_day.csv` & `city_day.csv`: Daily historical rollups for validation.
   - Cleaned demo window: 30 days of hourly telemetry across 5 core cities (**Coimbatore, Chennai, Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai**) covering 25 primary monitoring zones.
2. **Synthetic Data Generation (Reproducible, Seed = 42):**
   - **Zones (`data/synthetic/zones.csv`):** Stations mapped to zones (`zone_id = StationId`, `zone_name = StationName`). Realistic coordinates generated by city centroid + bounded Gaussian jitter ($\pm 0.05^{\circ}$). All rows labeled with `source="synthetic"`.
   - **Meteorology (`data/synthetic/weather.csv`):** Hourly ambient temperature ($22^{\circ}\text{C} - 34^{\circ}\text{C}$), relative humidity ($30\% - 95\%$), wind speed ($1.5 - 18 \text{ km/h}$), wind direction ($0^{\circ} - 360^{\circ}$), and barometric pressure ($1013 \pm 8\text{ hPa}$).
     - *Physical Stagnation Model:* Wind speed is inversely correlated with particulate levels ($PM_{2.5}, PM_{10}$) to realistically simulate atmospheric inversions where air stagnation exacerbates pollution.
   - **Citizen Symptom Reports (`data/synthetic/symptom_reports.json`):**
     - Thousands of records with fields `report_id`, `city`, `zone_id`, `timestamp`, `symptoms[]` (`breathing difficulty`, `cough`, `eye irritation`, `throat irritation`, `headache`, `chest tightness`, `skin irritation`), `severity` (1–5), `free_text`, `age_group`, `source="synthetic"`.
     - *Lagged Correlation:* Daily complaint volume per zone correlates with that zone's AQI with a 0 to 1-day lag plus stochastic noise.
     - *Outbreak Episodes:* Spikes injected in `CBE_ZN_01` (SIDCO Industrial) and `DEL_ZN_01` (Anand Vihar) where rising AQI coincides with sharp symptom surges.
     - *Decoy Cases:* Injected in `BOM_ZN_01` (high AQI due to maritime salt/dust but low respiratory complaints) and `CHE_ZN_02` (low AQI with transient complaint spike) to demonstrate that the fusion engine prevents both false positives and missed alerts.
   - **Social Media Telemetry (`data/synthetic/social_posts.json`):** Curated posts with city, zone, timestamps, complaint keywords, and sentiment scores.

## 3. Big Data Flow & Partitioning Conventions

1. **HDFS Raw Partitioning:**
   - Raw data lands into dual-partitioned directories:
     - `/data/raw/aqi/city=<c>/date=<YYYY-MM-DD>/aqi_data.csv`
     - `/data/raw/weather/city=<c>/date=<YYYY-MM-DD>/weather_data.csv`
     - `/data/raw/complaints/city=<c>/date=<YYYY-MM-DD>/complaints.csv`
2. **Hive External Tables:**
   - `aqi_readings` and `weather_data` map directly over HDFS raw partitions.
   - Hive dynamic partitioning is enabled (`hive.exec.dynamic.partition=true`, `hive.exec.max.dynamic.partitions=2000`).
   - Daily aggregations are generated via MapReduce and stored in `/data/processed/daily_aqi_agg` and `/data/processed/daily_pollutant_agg`.
3. **MongoDB Aggregation Pipeline:**
   - Flexible documents in `symptom_reports` and `social_posts` are processed via a multi-stage aggregation pipeline grouping by `(zone_id, date)`.
   - Computes normalized complaint density:
     $$\text{density} = \min\left(1.0, \frac{\text{complaints\_today}}{\text{baseline\_complaints\_per\_day} \times 4.0}\right)$$
   - Exports TSV to HDFS at `/data/processed/complaint_agg` for Apache Pig consumption.
4. **Apache Pig Fusion Join:**
   - Executes a `LEFT OUTER JOIN` on `(zone_id, date)` over MapReduce. Missing complaint records are coalesced to 0 count and 0.0 density.
   - Outputs final fused risk scores to `/data/processed/zone_risk_score`, immediately accessible to Hive external table `zone_risk_score`.

## 4. Health Risk Formula & Tier Escalation Logic

1. **Documented Formula:**
   $$\text{aqi\_score} = \min\left(\frac{\text{avg\_aqi}}{500}, 1.0\right) \times 100$$
   $$\text{complaint\_score} = \min(\text{complaint\_density\_norm}, 1.0) \times 100$$
   $$\text{trend\_score} = \text{scaled 3-day rise in AQI and complaint count (0–100)}$$
   $$\text{Composite Risk} = 0.50 \times \text{aqi\_score} + 0.35 \times \text{complaint\_score} + 0.15 \times \text{trend\_score}$$
2. **Base Risk Tiers:**
   - **GREEN (0–24.99):** Low Risk / Clean Air.
   - **YELLOW (25.0–49.99):** Moderate Alert.
   - **ORANGE (50.0–74.99):** High Health Advisory.
   - **RED (75.0–100.0):** Severe Emergency.
3. **Escalation Rule:**
   - If $\text{aqi\_score} \ge 50.0$ AND $\text{complaint\_score} \ge 40.0$ in the same zone, the zone is flagged as an **Escalated Outbreak Zone** (`escalated = true`), and its alert tier is promoted by one step:
     $$\text{GREEN} \to \text{YELLOW}, \quad \text{YELLOW} \to \text{ORANGE}, \quad \text{ORANGE} \to \text{RED}, \quad \text{RED} \to \text{RED}$$
   - Both `base_level`, `final_level`, and `escalated` flag are recorded in the metastore.

## 6. Null and Missing Value Handling

- **Particulate Gaps in Kaggle Data:** Stations with intermittent sensor dropouts are imputed using diurnal average cycles per city to prevent partition drops.
- **Missing Complaint Reports:** Zones with 0 complaints on a given date default to count 0, density 0.0, and severity 0.0, allowing Pig's `LEFT OUTER JOIN` to safely evaluate physical AQI alone without NaN contamination.
- **Stale Sensors:** Stations with missing readings for $>3$ consecutive hours are flagged with grey status `"SENSOR STALE / DATA UNAVAILABLE"` and never falsely defaulted to Green.
